The gate blocked our own fallback template
During the final audit, a bug made the deterministic template cite two evidence ids the frozen bundle no longer held. Gate 2 rejected it. We had documented that template as "cannot fail by construction" — it was unfailable by luck. Both the builder and the docstring were corrected.
A gate that only ever passes what we produce is not a gate.
