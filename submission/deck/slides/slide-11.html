Where the value comes from — four mechanisms, each tied to something already built. No ROI figure is claimed anywhere in this project.
Faster diagnosis
Evidence gathering and structured root-cause analysis are automated: warehouse, tickets, CRM and deploy logs reconciled in one pass.
Safer decisions
Unsupported explanations are blocked before delivery, and genuine uncertainty is distinguished from system failure rather than smoothed over.
Governed action
Recommendations are constrained to approved levers with named owners. Automation is bounded: raise a request, never execute a remediation.
Auditability
Every conclusion carries its evidence, its lineage and its decision state — 15 lineage records per run, every read audited including denials.
A good analyst does everything in that table. What they cannot do is run in fifteen seconds, apply the same materiality rule every time, or leave an audit trail that survives their absence. This product does not compete with a spreadsheet — it competes with how long the spreadsheet takes.
