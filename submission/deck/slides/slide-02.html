02 The obvious fix is the dangerous one
"The capability you want for root-cause analysis actively works against the humility you need."
A model that reasons
A 2026 fidelity audit found an LLM inverted the risk direction on 3 of 4 factors in a credit-risk narrative. Its priors overrode the supplied evidence — under constrained prompting.
A model that will not stop
Reasoning-tuned models abstain 24% worse than their non-reasoning counterparts (AbstentionBench). The better it reasons, the less it admits it cannot.
An industry that has named it
Gartner names "agent drift" as the risk in autonomous analytics and prescribes guardian layers as the mitigation.
If the default behaviour of a capable model is to answer confidently, abstention is not a feature you add later — it is an architecture you start from.
