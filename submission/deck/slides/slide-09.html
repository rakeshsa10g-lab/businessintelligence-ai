The result we are proudest of is a set of numbers that got worse
A realism audit found the corpus held only 13 distinct texts across 895 records — retrieval was close to a lookup. Widened to 30: every score fell. BM25 p@5 0.810 → 0.552 · recall@10 0.957 → 0.654 · dense recall@10 0.933 → 0.778 · RRF MRR 0.964 → 0.838. The lower numbers are the ones we report — and the first evidence the hybrid retriever earns its place.
