The result we are proudest of is a set of numbers that got worse
A realism audit found the corpus held only 13 distinct texts across 895 records — retrieval was close to a lookup. Widened to 30 and every score fell: BM25 recall@10 0.957 → 0.654, dense 0.933 → 0.778. The lower numbers are the ones we report. What they support: corpus realism removed the lexical shortcut, and dense retrieval became the stronger method under paraphrase (0.778 vs 0.654 recall@10). What they do not support: that fusion beats dense — RRF recall@10 is 0.697, between the two. Hybrid stays as a robustness mechanism, not as a measured win.
